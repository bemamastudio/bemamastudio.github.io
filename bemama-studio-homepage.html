About us

Bemama Studio is a small Canadian design studio based in Québec, run by Karina — a mom of three. We create digital party stationery: editable Canva templates for baby showers, first birthdays, baptisms and seasonal celebrations, including interactive website invitations with RSVP tracking.

Every design is created and finished by hand in our studio and delivered as an instant download through our Etsy shop. Our customers personalize the templates in Canva and print them at home or at a local print shop.
