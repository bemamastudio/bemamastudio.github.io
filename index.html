Privacy Policy — Bemama Studio

Effective date: October 23, 2026  |  Last updated: October 23, 2026

Bemama Studio ("we", "our", "us") is a small business based in Québec, Canada, that sells digital party stationery through its Etsy shop (bemamastudio.etsy.com). This Privacy Policy describes how our internal application BemamaStudioPins (the "App") accesses and uses information through the Pinterest API.

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private, internal tool. It is not offered to the public and has a single authorized user: the owner of Bemama Studio. It is used exclusively to manage the Pinterest business account @bemamastudio, which belongs to the same owner.

2. Information the App accesses

Through the Pinterest API, and only after the account owner authorizes access via Pinterest's official OAuth flow, the App may access:

  • The owner's Pinterest account ID and username;
  • The owner's boards (names and IDs) and Pins;
  • Basic analytics for the owner's own Pins and account.

The App is also authorized to create Pins and boards on the owner's own account, using product images, titles, descriptions and links that the owner selects in advance from her own Etsy product catalog.

3. How information is used

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to operate the tool for the owner:

  • To publish Pins of Bemama Studio's own products to the owner's own Pinterest boards;
  • To organize those Pins across the owner's boards;
  • To review the performance of the owner's own Pins.

The App does not like, follow, comment, send messages, or perform any other social interaction. It does not collect information about other Pinterest users.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose Pinterest data to any third party.
  • We do not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not collect or store Pinterest login credentials; access is exclusively via OAuth tokens issued by Pinterest.
  • We do not scrape Pinterest or access data outside the official Pinterest API.

5. Data storage and security

The App stores only the minimum technical information needed to function: the owner's OAuth access and refresh tokens and a publishing log (Pin IDs, board IDs, dates) of the owner's own content. Tokens are stored securely, are never shared, and can be revoked by the owner at any time from her Pinterest account settings, which immediately ends the App's access.

6. Data retention and deletion

Publishing logs are kept only while useful for operating the tool. The owner may request deletion of any stored information at any time by contacting us at the email below; deletion will be completed within 30 days.

7. Third-party services

Pins created by the App link to Bemama Studio's own listings on Etsy. Etsy's own privacy policy applies to visits and purchases made on Etsy.

8. Children

The App is a business tool for the account owner and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

Any update to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
